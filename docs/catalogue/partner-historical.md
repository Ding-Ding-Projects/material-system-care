# Partner and historical appendix

This appendix is deliberately separate from the IObit-owned catalogue. A featured-product recommendation is not evidence of IObit ownership, an included license, a bundled engine or an implemented equivalent.

## Featured partner products

The official [Advanced SystemCare manual](https://www.iobit.com/product-manuals/asc-help/) lists independent featured products, including iTop VPN, iTop Data Recovery, DualSafe Password Manager and iTop Private Browser. They remain partner references. Their possible independent equivalents are, respectively, an explicitly configured network provider, a real read-only recovery engine, a local encrypted credential vault, and a separately developed browser. None is implemented by this documentation inventory. No partner installation, sign-in, license purchase, browser replacement or data upload is authorized by listing them.

Other iTop products discovered on current partner pages, such as screen recording or PDF tooling, require a separate reviewed capability expansion rather than silently inheriting IObit ownership. The product's own screen-recording and document-conversion contracts remain independent.

## Historical Razer lineage

[Razer Cortex](https://www.razer.com/cortex) is a Razer product. Historical Razer Game Booster references may explain the lineage of game optimization, but they do not add a current IObit-owned product or engine to this catalogue. Current Cortex features and services, including launcher, performance optimization and commercial content, require separate current source review before an equivalence claim. The suite may offer an independent local game-launch profile with measured effects, but must not claim licensed Razer behavior, rewards, account integration or guaranteed FPS gains.

## Remaining source limitations

Product pages and manuals change independently and can disagree on version numbers. The 2026-10-08 baseline fixes the declared IDs and reviewed source links; it is not a promise that later vendor marketing remains unchanged. Some historical pages describe operating systems older than Windows 11. Their behaviors are either adapted through supported Windows 11 APIs or retained as explicit exclusions. All engine-, provider-, dataset-, elevation- and hardware-dependent gaps remain unresolved until implemented and verified.
